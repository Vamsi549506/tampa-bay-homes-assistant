Neighborhoods

  • Downtown Tampa: high-rise condos near the Riverwalk and business district.
  • South Tampa: established areas like Hyde Park near Bayshore Boulevard, with higher prices.
  • Westchase: a planned community popular with families.
  • Brandon: a large suburb east of Tampa with more affordable homes.
  • Wesley Chapel: a fast-growing Pasco County area with many new homes.

Market Data

  • Median listing price: $269,900 (2017) to $397,450 (2026), a 47% increase
  • Price peak: $449,000 in 2023
  • Active listings: 18,292 in 2026, up from a low of 4,662 in 2021
  • Median days on market: 72 days in 2026, up from 29 days in 2022

Sources: Realtor.com data published by FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), and Redfin data for the Tampa metro area.
